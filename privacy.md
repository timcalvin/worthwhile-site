# Worthwhile Privacy Policy

*Effective October 2026*

Worthwhile is a net worth tracker. It's built so your financial information stays yours.

## What Worthwhile collects

Nothing. Worthwhile has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The developer can't see your balances, accounts, or any other data you enter.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored on your devices and synced through **your own iCloud account** (Apple's CloudKit private database), so it appears on your Mac, iPad, and iPhone. Apple stores that data under your Apple ID according to [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). If you turn off iCloud for Worthwhile, your data stays only on that device.

Widgets read a small summary (your net worth and monthly change) that the app saves in a shared space on your device. It never leaves your device except through your own iCloud backups.

## Purchases

Worthwhile Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. Worthwhile only learns whether Pro is unlocked; it never sees your payment details.

## Notifications

If you turn on the month-end reminder or milestone alerts, they're scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

## CSV files

When you import or export a CSV, Worthwhile reads or writes only the file you choose, where you choose it.

## Children

Worthwhile isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
